# Decisions

Choices that had a real alternative, where the reasoning is not recoverable by reading
the code.

- [What the wire serves, and what a host imports](what-the-wire-serves-and-what-a-host-imports.md) - the property that decides whether a published name gets a tool row, and the three that stay in process
- [Both servers prove admission here](both-servers-prove-admission-here.md) - two conformance lanes in this repository's CI, at one pin, neither needing a VEuPathDB account
- [The root package holds what both servers share](the-root-package-holds-what-both-servers-share.md) - why a version module alone does not keep the WDK credential path out of the research process
- [A frozen step is keyed by the account it lands in](a-frozen-step-is-keyed-by-the-account-it-lands-in.md) - why the cache of materialized gene-set steps keys by the request's own WDK token
- [A listing page never outranks a paper](a-listing-page-never-outranks-a-paper.md) - relevance sorts by what a record is (identified and described, identified, an article, the rest) before its title score, and why weights, a venue list and citation counts were not the rule.
- [A keyed engine answers first and prices the call](a-keyed-engine-answers-first-and-prices-the-call.md) - deprecated: the Brave Search API and the price on the answer are removed, because the deployment runs its own SearXNG.
- [A metasearch the deployment runs answers first](a-metasearch-the-deployment-runs-answers-first.md) - SearXNG before the scraped engines, measured: 11 of 12 refused queries answered at 0.4 s, no key, no cost.
- [A control test files every id it was given](a-control-test-files-every-id-it-was-given.md) - two lists per control set, every count computed from them, both runners reading only an intersection with the controls (the step runner through a copy of the step), bounded per control id, every step and strategy they write deleted whatever ends the run, a unique strategy name per run and a sweep that deletes only leftovers older than two days, and why a sample, a cap with a `truncated` flag, a count-only answer, a read of every step record, a count with a refusal above a limit and a sweep by name alone were rejected.
- [An experiment is indexed per site](an-experiment-is-indexed-per-site.md) - other sites' dataset records as cards in one index per site, ranked and read by two functions, and why a merged index, indexed searches and a composed `search_for_searches` were rejected.
- [A separation is measured on the site](a-separation-is-measured-on-the-site.md) - every candidate scored by a WDK count, each candidate assembled by the control set it beats chance in (covering and intersecting, or subtracted), a greedy assembly confirmed by one read of the assembled tree, a request budget charged from measured counts, one dataset and one intersection for both control sets, and the scoring, listing and search shapes that were rejected.
- [An input-step search is listed whatever its question set](an-input-step-search-is-listed-whatever-its-question-set.md) - every catalog listing offers the searches that take an input step, the boolean question is left out by its shape, and why the question set, the name prefix and a filtered index were rejected.
- [A vocabulary lookup reads the phrasings of a term](a-vocabulary-lookup-reads-the-phrasings-of-a-term.md) - a term as a phrase with hyphen and space alike, as its words in any order, word by word only when neither matches, several phrasings at once, and a narrowed list that travels whole up to 300 entries with the total it was cut from.
- [A dataset search runs on its dataset's organism](a-dataset-search-runs-on-its-datasets-organism.md) - the organisms of the one dataset whose References table names the search, read from the AllDatasets report the build already posts, and why the question's `properties.organisms`, a per-search `DatasetsByQuestionName` read, the `Version` table and a union over datasets were rejected.
- [A curated search runs on its dataset's assay](a-curated-search-runs-on-its-datasets-assay.md) - the category of the one dataset whose References table names the search, and of the dataset record a study is, else the record's type, and why the search name, the question's `properties`, the coarse type alone and the EDA study metadata were rejected.
- [Each image is the last stage of its own Dockerfile](each-image-is-the-last-stage-of-its-own-dockerfile.md) - the shared Jenkins builder takes no target and reads only a semver tag, so the research server has `Dockerfile.research` and a release tag is spelled in semver, and why a target, a build argument, a derived image and a tag translation were rejected.
- [Tests run against QA, and a deployment names its site list](tests-run-against-qa-and-a-deployment-names-its-sites.md) - every test and recorder reads the QA list the client ships, production recordings left the tests, `check-test-sites.mjs` refuses a production or beta host, and why production, beta and a default list were rejected.
- [An expensive search holds one line on the database](an-expensive-search-holds-one-line-on-the-database.md) - the advisory-lock gate a deployment shares, installed by the served process at startup and by a host in its own, one search turn per served call, and why a line per process, a refusal and Redis were rejected.
- [The catalog shares a search definition across logins](the-catalog-shares-a-search-definition-across-logins.md) - one copy per site for every login, while a step write reads its definitions once per request, turn or job under one login; each site release is checked for a gene search whose vocabulary takes `user_id`; and why a per-request catalog and a copy per login were rejected.
- [An audit check reads its genome from the site](an-audit-check-reads-its-genome-from-the-site.md) - the two served gene-model checks take gene ids, read the gene rows, the gene record and a JBrowse genome window themselves, refuse an input the site cannot give, or a window over the limit, as a tool error, and run at most two checks at a time in worker threads; and why sequences passed by the caller and a verdict for a missing input were rejected.
- [A named gene is read by its record](a-named-gene-is-read-by-its-record.md) - a gene record read per named gene and a transcript record read per protein, the text search for an id no record names, the Orthologs table as the whole group, one id-list read per host run, and the intron check's staged peptide, motif and depth reads; and why a dataset per 150 ids, `GenesByOrthologs` and a protein attribute on the gene record were rejected.
- [A search note fills only an empty description](a-search-note-fills-only-an-empty-description.md) - a measured note per site and search, put in a search's description on every catalog and definition read only when the site sends none, the one note on `GroupsBySingleCopy`, the zero, precedence and length rules the expression help states, the one served builder that writes any expression from a typed pattern in place of a builder per question shape, the build that triggers a new measurement, and why hiding the search and replacing a site description were rejected.
- [A step read is bounded by its caller](a-step-read-is-bounded-by-its-caller.md) - the ids of a built step are read up to a limit the caller passes, a larger step raises `StepTooLargeError`, a site refusal is never an empty step, every other record read is bounded by its input, and why a read of every record, a cut at the limit, one library cap and an empty step for a failed read were rejected.
- [A served result validates its own dump](a-served-result-validates-its-own-dump.md) - every model a served tool returns reads the JSON its by-alias dump writes, a field read from a site column under another name lists its camelCase alias beside it, a field that decodes a site value takes the value it dumps, and why private row models, the site's column names on the wire and a package alias policy were rejected.
- [A compare past one expression is computed from group clouds](a-compare-past-one-expression-is-computed-from-group-clouds.md) - a compare past the expression caps reads each set's per-group protein counts from the sequence word cloud, sliced under 10,000 groups and at most 24 requests, and answers a snapshot; raising the caps, banded expressions and per-group table reads were rejected.
- [A radio pair offers a term as two readings](a-radio-pair-offers-a-term-as-two-readings.md) - the entries whose label names a term with the free text off, and the free-text wildcard with the vocabulary half at its declared `N/A` entry, never one binding, each counted on its own; and the count of accession entries a name cannot reach.
- [A Decisions choice widens the search candidates and doubts a vocabulary pick](a-decisions-choice-widens-the-candidates-and-doubts-a-vocabulary-pick.md) - behind `DECISIONS_ENABLED`, a Choice over every offered search appends its picks after the ranking, and a narrowed read carries the picked entry with a confidence a fit predicate caps; off or failing, both reads are unchanged; and why Jev through OpenRouter, free-text Luna picks and the Choice confidence alone were rejected.
